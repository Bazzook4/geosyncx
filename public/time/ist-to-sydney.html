India to Sydney time difference

Sydney is 4.5 hours ahead of India Standard Time (IST) during AEST, and 5.5 hours ahead during daylight saving (AEDT). When it's noon in India, it's 4:30 PM or 5:30 PM in Sydney.

IST to Sydney: Sydney is 5.5 hours ahead of India in winter, and Sydney is 4.5 hours ahead of India in summer.

Sydney observes daylight saving (AEDT) from early October to early April, the opposite season to London and New York.

Frequently asked questions

What is the time difference between India and Sydney?

Sydney is 5.5 hours ahead of India in winter and 4.5 hours ahead of India in summer. Sydney is 4.5 hours ahead of India Standard Time (IST) during AEST, and 5.5 hours ahead during daylight saving (AEDT). When it's noon in India, it's 4:30 PM or 5:30 PM in Sydney.

What time is it in Sydney when it is 9:00 AM in India?

When it is 9:00 AM in India, it is 2:30 PM in Sydney in winter and 1:30 PM in summer.

What is the best time for a call between India and Sydney?

Indian morning (6:30 AM - 11:30 AM IST) overlaps with Sydney late morning to afternoon.

Does the IST to Sydney difference change during the year?

Sydney observes daylight saving (AEDT) from early October to early April, the opposite season to London and New York.

What is 2 PM IST in Sydney time?

2:00 PM IST is 7:30 PM in Sydney in winter and 6:30 PM in summer.

What is 6 PM IST in Sydney time?

6:00 PM IST is 11:30 PM in Sydney in winter and 10:30 PM in summer.

What is the IST offset?

India Standard Time (IST) is UTC+5:30 (IANA zone Asia/Kolkata). India does not observe daylight saving time, so IST never changes.
