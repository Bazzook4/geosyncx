India to GMT time difference

India Standard Time (IST) is exactly 5 hours and 30 minutes ahead of Greenwich Mean Time (GMT/UTC). This offset never changes as neither IST nor UTC observes daylight saving time.

IST to GMT: GMT is 5.5 hours behind India all year round.

Neither IST nor GMT/UTC observes Daylight Saving Time, so this conversion is consistent year-round.

Best time to call: GMT timing from India

GMT is often used as a reference point. For business hours (9 AM - 5 PM GMT), that's 2:30 PM - 10:30 PM IST.

Tips

  • GMT and UTC are effectively the same for most purposes
  • Many global platforms use UTC for timestamps
  • When in doubt, convert to GMT first, then to local time

Who uses IST to GMT conversion

  • Converting to universal time for global events
  • Scheduling international conferences
  • Setting up server timestamps
  • Coordinating across multiple global timezones
