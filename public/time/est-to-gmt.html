Frequently asked questions

What is the time difference between New York and GMT?

GMT is 5 hours ahead of New York in winter and 4 hours ahead of New York in summer. Eastern Time is 5 hours behind GMT/UTC. When it's 9 AM in New York, it's 2:00 PM GMT.

What time is it in GMT when it is 9:00 AM in New York?

When it is 9:00 AM in New York, it is 2:00 PM in GMT in winter and 1:00 PM in summer.

What is the best time for a call between New York and GMT?

EST morning (9 AM - 12 PM) is GMT afternoon (2 PM - 5 PM).

Does the EST to GMT difference change during the year?

Yes. Daylight saving changes shift the gap between 5 and 4 hours.

What is 2 PM EST in GMT time?

2:00 PM EST is 7:00 PM in GMT in winter and 6:00 PM in summer.

What is 6 PM EST in GMT time?

6:00 PM EST is 11:00 PM in GMT in winter and 10:00 PM in summer.
