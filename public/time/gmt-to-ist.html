Frequently asked questions

What is the time difference between GMT and India?

India is 5.5 hours ahead of GMT. GMT/UTC is 5.5 hours behind India Standard Time. When it's noon GMT, it's 5:30 PM in India.

What time is it in India when it is 9:00 AM in GMT?

When it is 9:00 AM in GMT, it is 2:30 PM in India.

What is the best time for a call between GMT and India?

GMT business hours (9 AM - 5 PM) correspond to IST 2:30 PM - 10:30 PM.

Does the GMT to IST difference change during the year?

No. The difference is constant all year.

What is 2 PM GMT in IST time?

2:00 PM GMT is 7:30 PM in India.

What is 6 PM GMT in IST time?

6:00 PM GMT is 11:30 PM in India.

What is the IST offset?

India Standard Time (IST) is UTC+5:30 (IANA zone Asia/Kolkata). India does not observe daylight saving time, so IST never changes.
