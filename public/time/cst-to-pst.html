Frequently asked questions

What is the time difference between Chicago and Los Angeles?

Los Angeles is 2 hours behind Chicago. Central Time is 2 hours ahead of Pacific Time. When it's noon in Chicago, it's 10:00 AM in Los Angeles.

What time is it in Los Angeles when it is 9:00 AM in Chicago?

When it is 9:00 AM in Chicago, it is 7:00 AM in Los Angeles.

What is the best time for a call between Chicago and Los Angeles?

Excellent overlap with just 2 hours difference.

Does the CST to PST difference change during the year?

No. The difference is constant all year.

What is 2 PM CST in PST time?

What is 6 PM CST in PST time?
