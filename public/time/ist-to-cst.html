India to Chicago time difference

India Standard Time (IST) is 11.5 hours ahead of Central Standard Time (CST). When it's noon in India, it's 12:30 AM in Chicago/Dallas.

IST to CST: Chicago is 11.5 hours behind India in winter, and Chicago is 10.5 hours behind India in summer.

During Central Daylight Time (CDT), the difference reduces to 10.5 hours.

Frequently asked questions

What is the time difference between India and Chicago?

Chicago is 11.5 hours behind India in winter and 10.5 hours behind India in summer. India Standard Time (IST) is 11.5 hours ahead of Central Standard Time (CST). When it's noon in India, it's 12:30 AM in Chicago/Dallas.

What time is it in Chicago when it is 9:00 AM in India?

When it is 9:00 AM in India, it is 9:30 PM in Chicago in winter and 10:30 PM in summer.

What is the best time for a call between India and Chicago?

The best overlap is 7:30 PM - 10:30 PM IST (8:00 AM - 11:00 AM CST).

Does the IST to CST difference change during the year?

During Central Daylight Time (CDT), the difference reduces to 10.5 hours.

What is 2 PM IST in CST time?

2:00 PM IST is 2:30 AM in Chicago in winter and 3:30 AM in summer.

What is 6 PM IST in CST time?

6:00 PM IST is 6:30 AM in Chicago in winter and 7:30 AM in summer.

What is the IST offset?

India Standard Time (IST) is UTC+5:30 (IANA zone Asia/Kolkata). India does not observe daylight saving time, so IST never changes.
