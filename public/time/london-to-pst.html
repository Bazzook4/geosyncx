London to Los Angeles time difference

London is 8 hours ahead of Los Angeles (Pacific Time). When it's noon in London, it's 4:00 AM in California. This makes real-time collaboration challenging.

London to PST: Los Angeles is 8 hours behind London all year round.

Both observe DST but on different schedules, causing brief periods of 7 or 9 hour differences.

What is London time?
