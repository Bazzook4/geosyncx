India to Dubai time difference

Dubai (Gulf Standard Time) is 1 hour 30 minutes behind India Standard Time. When it's noon in India, it's 10:30 AM in Dubai. Neither observes daylight saving time.

IST to Dubai: Dubai is 1.5 hours behind India all year round.

Neither India nor the UAE observes Daylight Saving Time, so the 1.5 hour gap is constant all year.
