Frequently asked questions

What is the time difference between GMT and New York?

New York is 5 hours behind GMT in winter and 4 hours behind GMT in summer. GMT/UTC is 5 hours ahead of Eastern Standard Time. When it's noon GMT, it's 7:00 AM in New York.

What time is it in New York when it is 9:00 AM in GMT?

When it is 9:00 AM in GMT, it is 4:00 AM in New York in winter and 5:00 AM in summer.

What is the best time for a call between GMT and New York?

GMT afternoon aligns with EST morning business hours.

Does the GMT to EST difference change during the year?

Yes. Daylight saving changes shift the gap between -5 and -4 hours.

What is 2 PM GMT in EST time?

2:00 PM GMT is 9:00 AM in New York in winter and 10:00 AM in summer.

What is 6 PM GMT in EST time?

6:00 PM GMT is 1:00 PM in New York in winter and 2:00 PM in summer.
