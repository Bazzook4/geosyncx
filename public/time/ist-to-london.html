India to London time difference

India Standard Time (IST) is 5 hours and 30 minutes ahead of Greenwich Mean Time (GMT). When it's noon in India, it's 6:30 AM in London. During British Summer Time (BST), the difference reduces to 4.5 hours.

IST to London: London is 5.5 hours behind India in winter, and London is 4.5 hours behind India in summer.

London observes Daylight Saving Time (BST) from late March to late October, reducing the time difference to 4.5 hours.

Frequently asked questions

What is the time difference between India and London?

London is 5.5 hours behind India in winter and 4.5 hours behind India in summer. India Standard Time (IST) is 5 hours and 30 minutes ahead of Greenwich Mean Time (GMT). When it's noon in India, it's 6:30 AM in London. During British Summer Time (BST), the difference reduces to 4.5 hours.

What time is it in London when it is 9:00 AM in India?

When it is 9:00 AM in India, it is 3:30 AM in London in winter and 4:30 AM in summer.

What is the best time for a call between India and London?

The best overlap for business calls is 1:30 PM - 6:30 PM IST (8:00 AM - 1:00 PM London). This ensures both parties are within standard working hours.

Does the IST to London difference change during the year?

London observes Daylight Saving Time (BST) from late March to late October, reducing the time difference to 4.5 hours.

What is 2 PM IST in UK time?

2:00 PM IST is 8:30 AM in London in winter and 9:30 AM in summer.

What is 6 PM IST in UK time?

6:00 PM IST is 12:30 PM in London in winter and 1:30 PM in summer.

What is the IST offset?

India Standard Time (IST) is UTC+5:30 (IANA zone Asia/Kolkata). India does not observe daylight saving time, so IST never changes.

What is London time?

London uses Greenwich Mean Time (GMT, UTC+0) in winter and British Summer Time (BST, UTC+1) from the last Sunday of March to the last Sunday of October.
