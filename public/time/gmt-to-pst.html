Frequently asked questions

What is the time difference between GMT and Los Angeles?

Los Angeles is 8 hours behind GMT in winter and 7 hours behind GMT in summer. GMT/UTC is 8 hours ahead of Pacific Standard Time. When it's noon GMT, it's 4:00 AM in Los Angeles.

What time is it in Los Angeles when it is 9:00 AM in GMT?

When it is 9:00 AM in GMT, it is 1:00 AM in Los Angeles in winter and 2:00 AM in summer.

What is the best time for a call between GMT and Los Angeles?

GMT late afternoon (4 PM - 6 PM) is early morning on the US West Coast (8 AM - 10 AM).

Does the GMT to PST difference change during the year?

Yes. Daylight saving changes shift the gap between -8 and -7 hours.

What is 2 PM GMT in PST time?

2:00 PM GMT is 6:00 AM in Los Angeles in winter and 7:00 AM in summer.

What is 6 PM GMT in PST time?
