Frequently asked questions

What is the time difference between Chicago and India?

India is 11.5 hours ahead of Chicago in winter and 10.5 hours ahead of Chicago in summer. Central Time is 11.5 hours behind India Standard Time. When it's noon in Chicago, it's 11:30 PM in India.

What time is it in India when it is 9:00 AM in Chicago?

When it is 9:00 AM in Chicago, it is 8:30 PM in India in winter and 7:30 PM in summer.

What is the best time for a call between Chicago and India?

CST morning (8-10 AM) aligns with IST evening (7:30-9:30 PM).

Does the CST to IST difference change during the year?

Yes. Daylight saving changes shift the gap between 11.5 and 10.5 hours.

What is the IST offset?

India Standard Time (IST) is UTC+5:30 (IANA zone Asia/Kolkata). India does not observe daylight saving time, so IST never changes.
