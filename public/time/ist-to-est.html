India to New York time difference

India Standard Time (IST) is 10.5 hours ahead of Eastern Standard Time (EST). When it's noon in India, it's 1:30 AM in New York. The gap reduces to 9.5 hours during Eastern Daylight Time (EDT).

IST to EST: New York is 10.5 hours behind India in winter, and New York is 9.5 hours behind India in summer.

The US observes Daylight Saving Time from March to November, reducing the time difference to 9.5 hours during EDT.

Best time to call: New York timing from India

The limited overlap window is 7:00 PM - 10:30 PM IST (8:30 AM - 12:00 PM EST). Evening calls in India align with US morning business hours.

Tips

  • Morning standup calls often work at 8-9 PM IST
  • The US has multiple time zones - confirm which one your contact is in
  • Consider alternating meeting times to share the inconvenience

Frequently asked questions

What is the time difference between India and New York?

New York is 10.5 hours behind India in winter and 9.5 hours behind India in summer. India Standard Time (IST) is 10.5 hours ahead of Eastern Standard Time (EST). When it's noon in India, it's 1:30 AM in New York. The gap reduces to 9.5 hours during Eastern Daylight Time (EDT).

What time is it in New York when it is 9:00 AM in India?

When it is 9:00 AM in India, it is 10:30 PM in New York in winter and 11:30 PM in summer.

What is the best time for a call between India and New York?

The limited overlap window is 7:00 PM - 10:30 PM IST (8:30 AM - 12:00 PM EST). Evening calls in India align with US morning business hours.

Does the IST to EST difference change during the year?

The US observes Daylight Saving Time from March to November, reducing the time difference to 9.5 hours during EDT.

What is 2 PM IST in EST time?

2:00 PM IST is 3:30 AM in New York in winter and 4:30 AM in summer.

What is 6 PM IST in EST time?

6:00 PM IST is 7:30 AM in New York in winter and 8:30 AM in summer.

What is the IST offset?

India Standard Time (IST) is UTC+5:30 (IANA zone Asia/Kolkata). India does not observe daylight saving time, so IST never changes.
