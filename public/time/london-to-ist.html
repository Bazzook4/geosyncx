London to India time difference

London is 5.5 hours behind India Standard Time (IST). When it's noon in London, it's 5:30 PM in India. During British Summer Time (BST), the difference is 4.5 hours.

London to IST: India is 5.5 hours ahead of London in winter, and India is 4.5 hours ahead of London in summer.

UK observes British Summer Time (BST) from late March to late October.

Best time to call: India timing from London

UK morning (9 AM - 12 PM) aligns with India afternoon (2:30 PM - 5:30 PM) for optimal overlap.

Tips

  • India does not observe DST, so the offset changes when UK clocks change
  • London afternoon meetings mean late evening for India participants

Frequently asked questions

What is the time difference between London and India?

India is 5.5 hours ahead of London in winter and 4.5 hours ahead of London in summer. London is 5.5 hours behind India Standard Time (IST). When it's noon in London, it's 5:30 PM in India. During British Summer Time (BST), the difference is 4.5 hours.

What time is it in India when it is 9:00 AM in London?

When it is 9:00 AM in London, it is 2:30 PM in India in winter and 1:30 PM in summer.

What is the best time for a call between London and India?

UK morning (9 AM - 12 PM) aligns with India afternoon (2:30 PM - 5:30 PM) for optimal overlap.

Does the London to IST difference change during the year?

UK observes British Summer Time (BST) from late March to late October.

What is 2 PM London in IST time?

2:00 PM London is 7:30 PM in India in winter and 6:30 PM in summer.

What is 6 PM London in IST time?

6:00 PM London is 11:30 PM in India in winter and 10:30 PM in summer.

What is the IST offset?

India Standard Time (IST) is UTC+5:30 (IANA zone Asia/Kolkata). India does not observe daylight saving time, so IST never changes.

What is London time?

London uses Greenwich Mean Time (GMT, UTC+0) in winter and British Summer Time (BST, UTC+1) from the last Sunday of March to the last Sunday of October.
