India to San Francisco time difference

India Standard Time (IST) is 13.5 hours ahead of Pacific Standard Time (PST). When it's noon in India, it's 10:30 PM the previous day in California. This is one of the most challenging timezone pairs for real-time collaboration.

IST to PST: San Francisco is 13.5 hours behind India in winter, and San Francisco is 12.5 hours behind India in summer.

During Pacific Daylight Time (PDT) from March to November, the difference reduces to 12.5 hours.
