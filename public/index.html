Frequently asked questions

What is the time difference between India and the UK?

London is 5.5 hours behind India in winter (GMT) and 4.5 hours behind in summer (BST). India (IST, UTC+5:30) does not change its clocks, so the gap shifts when the UK changes clocks in late March and late October.
