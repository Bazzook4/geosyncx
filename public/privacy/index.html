Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

GeoSyncX (www.geosyncx.com) is a free set of timezone and productivity tools. This page explains what information is handled when you use the site.

What stays in your browser

The tools run in your browser. The cities you compare, your primary timezone, your theme, your to-do items and your meeting preferences are saved in your browser's local storage so they are there next time. This data is not sent to us. Text you paste into the JSON / XML Formatter is processed on your device and is not uploaded.

You can remove this data at any time by clearing the site data for geosyncx.com in your browser settings.

Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account and we do not collect names, email addresses or payment details through the site. If you email us at hello@geosyncx.com, we receive your email address and message and use them only to reply.

Like any website, our hosting provider may keep standard server logs (such as IP address, browser type and requested page) for security and reliability.

Advertising and cookies

We use Google AdSense to show ads. Google and its partners, as third-party vendors, use cookies and similar technologies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites.

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to GeoSyncX and other sites on the internet.
  • You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, or opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies at aboutads.info.
  • To learn how Google uses data from sites that use its services, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, you will be asked for your consent to cookies for advertising through a consent message. You can change your choice at any time using the privacy options link that message provides, or by clearing your cookies.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR or CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data held about you, and to object to certain processing. Since we hold very little personal data, the quickest route is to email us and we will respond.

Children

GeoSyncX is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email hello@geosyncx.com.
