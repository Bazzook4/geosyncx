How to compare timezones

  1. Search for a city or country and click it to add it.
  2. Star the timezone you work from to make it your primary one.
  3. Pick a date and time. The Equivalent time column shows that moment in every timezone.
  4. Remove rows with ✕. Your list is saved in your browser.

Frequently asked questions

How do I compare multiple timezones?

Search for a city or country, then click it to add it to the table. You can add as many timezones as you need and every clock updates live.

What does the star do?

The star sets a timezone as your primary one. When you enter a date and time, it is read in the primary timezone and converted for every other row.

How do I convert a specific time?

Star your primary timezone, then pick a date and time. The Equivalent time column shows that moment in each timezone, with a day marker when it falls on the next or previous day.

Does it account for daylight saving?

Yes. Conversions use the IANA timezone database for the date you choose, so the gap between, say, India and the UK changes between winter (5.5 hours) and summer (4.5 hours).

Are my selections saved?

Your timezones and primary timezone are saved in your browser's local storage. Nothing is sent to a server.
