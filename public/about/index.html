Why I built GeoSyncX

A tool born from real frustration.

I'm a sales executive working with leads from around the globe.

Every day I'd get inquiries with just a phone number: no location, no timezone. So I'd open one tab to look up the country code, another to check their timezone, and a third to figure out when to call.

Then came the multi-stakeholder deals. A prospect in London, their CTO in Singapore, and their US team in California. Finding a single meeting slot that didn't require someone to wake up at 3 AM took forever.

"I got tired of juggling 3-4 different tools for something so simple."

So I built GeoSyncX, everything I need in one place:

  • See a phone number? Instantly know where they're calling from with the Phone Code Lookup.
  • Need to compare timezones? Add as many as you want in Compare Timezones.
  • Scheduling a meeting? Find the best overlap in seconds with Best Meeting Time.
  • Converting a specific pair? Try IST to UK time or GMT to IST.

No signups. No clutter. Just the tools I wish I had from day one.
